ABOUT SKY BLUEPRINT

Built by a South African,
for South Africans.

Sky Blueprint was created to give every South African — whether you are a student, entrepreneur, job seeker or professional — access to powerful digital tools that big companies take for granted. We believe technology should be affordable, simple, and built for the African context.

Wongalethu Mkapu
Founder & Visionary

Sky Blueprint was founded by Wongalethu Mkapu, a young South African entrepreneur born on 20 August 2000. Driven by a deep belief that talent and ambition are evenly spread across South Africa — but opportunity is not — Wongalethu set out to close that gap.

He saw too many capable people held back simply because they could not afford a website, did not have the tools to build a professional CV, or struggled to manage the digital side of their lives and businesses. Sky Blueprint is his answer: a single, affordable platform that puts world-class digital tools into the hands of every South African, no matter their background.

As a member of the new generation of African founders, Wongalethu built Sky Blueprint on one principle — when you give people the right tools, you give them the power to build their own future.
